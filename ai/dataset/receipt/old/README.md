# Archived generated receipt images

The previous 30 selected synthetic PNGs were moved here from `../generated/` on
2026-10-29. Filenames and image bytes are unchanged. They cover ten recipes with
`enough`, `missing_some` and `below_half` variants; `below_half` means fewer than
half the required ingredients are present.

`manifest.json` preserves their hashes and recipe/scenario provenance. Image
paths are relative to this folder; `previous_image` records each former path
relative to `receipt/`. `expected` remains null: these are development fixtures,
not independently human-reviewed evaluation labels.

Detailed historical batch records remain local under the ignored
`../../work/receipts/20261024/`. The current selected 10 short, 10 medium and
10 long JPEGs are in `../generated/`, indexed by `../manifest.json`.
